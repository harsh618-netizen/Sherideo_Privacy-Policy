Sherideo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sherideo (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) may collect, use, store, and share information when you use our video-sharing app.

Information We May Collect

  • Account information: If you create an account, we may process your email address and authentication-related information to register you and sign you in. Do not share your password with anyone.
  • Videos and related details: If video-upload features are enabled and you upload content, the video and information you provide with it may be processed and stored so the app can provide its video-sharing features.
  • Technical information: Basic device, diagnostic, or usage information may be processed when required to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the app. Analytics or crash-reporting data is collected only if the corresponding services are enabled in the version you use.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide video-sharing features, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, improve app performance, and respond to support requests.

Storage and Third-Party Services

Sherideo is being developed using MIT App Inventor and may use Supabase for account authentication, database, or file-storage features. Information processed by a third-party provider is subject to that provider’s terms and privacy practices. We will update this policy if the services used by the published app change.

Do not assume that Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics is used unless those services are actually included and enabled in the app.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the app and its users. Videos you choose to share may be visible to other users according to the app’s features and settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the app, meet legitimate operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. To ask a question or request deletion of account information or uploaded content, contact us using the email below. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Children’s Privacy

Sherideo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The updated effective date will be shown at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email support.sherideo@gmail.com.
